How the clock and weather read

| starts a new row. Fields: {time} {date} {temp} {feels} {hi} {lo} {hum} {wind} {cond} {place} {units} {wind_units}. A field can carry its own format: {time:%H:%M}. To show one of these, choose it on the Messages page, or use the fields inside any message.
