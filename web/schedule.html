"Turn off" holds the display and the car's lights off, even through a restart, until "Turn on". Home Assistant will use the same switch. Turning on returns control to the sleep hours below.

Weekly schedule

When no rule matches, the sign shows what is set on the Messages page. Rules are checked top to bottom and the first match wins; one-off dates come before all of them. Times may run past midnight (22:00 to 06:00).

One-off dates

Sleep hours

A tap on the dark screen lights it for a few minutes. Until the clock is known (a minute after the very first start) the sign stays on rather than sitting dark.

Motion sensing

Uses the Tab5's front camera. Pictures are only taken while "Check the camera" is running, for aiming and tuning; red marks the parts of the picture that changed. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. Turning motion sensing on takes effect after a restart (the camera has to start before Wi-Fi). It needs some light: in a fully dark car the camera sees nothing. Switching the lights or display on or off is not counted as motion.

Screen and lights

The relay follows the display: on together, off together. Use an opto-isolated relay module (or M5Stack's Relay unit on Port A), never a bare relay coil on the pin; if the car's lights run on mains power, the relay and its wiring must be rated and enclosed for it.
