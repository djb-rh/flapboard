Motion sensing

The camera starts when the sign does. Restart to turn motion sensing on.

Uses the Tab5's front camera. Pictures are only taken while "Check the camera" is running, for aiming and tuning; red marks the parts of the picture that changed. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. It needs some light: in a fully dark car the camera sees nothing. Switching the lights or display on or off is not counted as motion. Sleep hours and the schedule are on the Schedule page.

Lights relay

The relay follows the display: on together, off together. Use an opto-isolated relay module (or M5Stack's Relay unit on Port A), never a bare relay coil on the pin; if the car's lights run on mains power, the relay and its wiring must be rated and enclosed for it.

Home Assistant (MQTT)

With Home Assistant's MQTT integration, the sign appears by itself: display and lights on/off, a message box, what to show, volume, brightness, sound, next message, motion and diagnostics. Leave the address empty to turn MQTT off. The password is never shown again; leave it blank to keep the saved one.

System

A backup holds the settings and message files. Passwords and photos are not included.

SD card

Formatting erases everything on the card and makes it FAT32, which this sign, Windows and macOS all read and write (Windows only refuses to format cards over 32 GB as FAT32). The sign restarts to do it; a 128 GB card takes under a minute.

Fonts and sounds

Extra fonts (.ttf or .otf) put here appear in the font list on the Messages page. Sounds: mono 16-bit WAV files named clack_*.wav replace the built-in clacks. Neither is needed; the sign has its own.
