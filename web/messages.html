What to show

The lines before go on the top rows and the lines after on the bottom rows, with the message centred between. They share the board with the message: keep your message paragraphs short enough to leave room for them, or the bottom lines of a long message won't show.

The clock and weather layouts are on the Clock & weather page; the slideshow on the Photos page. The Schedule page can switch between these by day and time.

Side pictures

Uploads are resized here in the browser to exactly fit their strip, so the sign draws them sharp. They are kept in messages/side-pictures (see Message files, below).
